Welcome to Brgy. San Nicolas!

Barangay San Nicolas is one of the barangays in San Pablo City, Laguna, in the Philippines. The history of Barangay San Nicolas is deeply tied to the larger history of San Pablo City, one of the oldest settlements in Laguna.

With a population of about 7,255 as of the 2020 Census, Barangay San Nicolas represents a significant part of the community. It has seen steady growth over the years, adapting to changing demographics.

Our committed officials focus on community development through infrastructure projects, youth engagement, and initiatives to support local businesses, prioritizing education, healthcare, and disaster preparedness.

Offering a vibrant community rich in culture and convenience, Barangay San Nicolas is strategically located near essential amenities, fostering a welcoming atmosphere where tradition meets modern living.
